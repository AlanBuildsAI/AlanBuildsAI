## How I Approach an Analysis

1. **Frame the decision** — clarify the stakeholder, business question, scope, and constraints.
2. **Audit the evidence** — inspect grain, missingness, duplicates, definitions, dates, and source limitations before calculating KPIs.
3. **Define metrics explicitly** — document denominators, cohorts, exclusions, and business meaning.
4. **Build reproducibly** — keep SQL and transformation logic reviewable and regenerate outputs from source data.
5. **Challenge the result** — test alternative definitions, cohort bias, data-quality issues, and unsupported interpretations.
6. **Communicate for action** — surface the decision, evidence, limitations, and next measurement step instead of overstating causality.
